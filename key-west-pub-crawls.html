Which Key West pub crawl should you book?

Six live crawls in three formats — on foot in Old Town, by van up through the Lower Keys, or by private boat. Here's what each costs, who it's for, and the age rules that actually get enforced.

Short answer

Book the Original Key West Pub Crawl at $39.95. It is the only one of the six that includes a drink at every stop — five bars, five drinks, a guide setting the pace, and a souvenir T-shirt — which makes it the best value once you would have bought two rounds anyway. It runs Tuesday, Friday and Saturday at 8:00pm from 424 Greene Street.

Going on a night it doesn't run, or watching the budget? The Bone Island Haunted crawl is $29.95 and runs six nights a week. Want the whole thing private for a bachelorette or a birthday? Skip to the bar crawls by boat.

FAQ

Key West Pub Crawl Questions

How much is a pub crawl in Key West?

Guided walking pub crawls run $29.95 to $39.95 per person. The Bone Island Haunted Pub Crawl is $29.95, the Key West Competitive Bar Crawl is $37.50, and the Original Key West Pub Crawl is $39.95 with a drink included at each of five bars. A tiki bar crawl by van through the Lower Keys is $83 per person. Private bar crawls by boat are priced for the whole boat rather than per seat: $495 for up to 6 people, or $1,295 for up to 8.

Do you have to be 21 to do a Key West pub crawl?

Yes for every walking crawl and for the van crawl — all four are 21 and over. Everyone in the party needs their own ticket, and the operators do not sell a reduced observer rate for a non-drinking guest. The private bar crawls by boat are the exception: the Six Fins Tiki Bar Crawl by Water is listed for ages 16 to 100, because it is a private charter rather than a bar-entry product.

Are drinks included in a Key West pub crawl?

On the Original Key West Pub Crawl, yes — a domestic beer or house-brand cocktail is included at each of the five bars, plus a souvenir T-shirt. The Bone Island Haunted crawl and the Competitive Bar Crawl include the guided route and the entertainment rather than the drinks themselves. On the Lower Keys van crawl you get happy-hour pricing at each of the three tiki bars. Private boat crawls do not include bar tabs.

How long is a Key West pub crawl?

Walking crawls run 2 to 2.5 hours: the Bone Island Haunted crawl is 2 hours, and both the Original and the Competitive crawl are 2.5 hours. The Lower Keys tiki bar crawl by van is 4 hours, because most of the extra time is driving between islands. Private boat crawls are 3 hours (Fun in the Sun) or 4 hours (Six Fins).

Where do Key West pub crawls meet?

The Original Key West Pub Crawl and the Bone Island Haunted Pub Crawl both meet at 424 Greene Street in Old Town, next door to Captain Tony's Saloon. The Competitive Bar Crawl meets a block away at Conch Republic Seafood Company, 631 Greene Street. The van crawl picks up in Key West and runs up through the Lower Keys to Marathon. The Six Fins boat crawl departs from Stock Island, at 7001 Shrimp Road.

What is the difference between a walking pub crawl and a bar crawl by boat?

A walking crawl is sold by the seat, so two people pay for two tickets and join a guided group of strangers. A bar crawl by boat is sold as a whole-boat private charter, so you pay one flat rate no matter how many of you there are, and no one outside your group is aboard. The boats reach waterfront tiki bars you cannot walk to. On cost per head the boats only make sense once your group is large: $495 across 6 people is about $83 each, and $1,295 across 8 is about $162 each.

Can you book a private pub crawl in Key West?

Yes, but the private options are the boat crawls rather than the walking ones. Fun in the Sun runs a captained Bar Crawl By Boat for up to 6 passengers at $495 for the boat, and Six Fins runs a 4-hour Tiki Bar Crawl by Water for up to 8 at $1,295, departing Stock Island. Both are private to your group, which makes them the usual pick for a bachelorette or a birthday.

Do Key West pub crawls have free cancellation?

The three walking crawls do — all three show free cancellation and instant confirmation on the live booking calendar. The van crawl does not: it takes cancellations by phone with 48 hours notice for a full refund or credit. The Six Fins boat crawl is non-refundable inside 7 days of departure. Always confirm the terms on the operator's calendar before you pay.
