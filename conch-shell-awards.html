The 2026 Conch Shell Awards

Every year we look at the Key West operators listed on this site and recognise the ones putting the most on the water. These are the 14 nominees for 2026.

How nominees were chosen

Nominations are drawn from our own catalogue, not from a public vote or a paid entry. We list 905 Key West tours from local operators. Nominees are the operators with the deepest catalogue in each category, measured on 4 September 2026.

  • No operator paid to be nominated, and none can.
  • Nomination does not affect where an operator appears in our search results.
  • Tour counts are what we actually list, and they move as operators add and retire trips.

Operator of the Year

  • Sunset Watersports 51 tours listed

    Parasailing, sunset sails, snorkeling and fishing — the widest range of any operator we list.

What happens next

  1. Nominees are invited to send a short note about what makes their operation different.
  2. Those notes are published on this page beside each nominee, in their own words.
  3. One winner per category is named at the end of the 2026 season.

Nominated and want to add yours, or think we have your tour count wrong? Email walktheplankadventures@gmail.com.
