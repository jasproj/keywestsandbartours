FAQ

Private Custom Charter Questions

What's included in a private custom charter?

Most include captain, fuel, safety equipment, and basic amenities. Premium vessels often include snorkel gear, paddleboards, floats, and water toys. Food/drinks are typically BYOB unless specified. Routing is customizable — sandbar stops, snorkel sites, sunset position, and timing are all up to your group.

Can you help with proposals?

Absolutely. Many captains have helped stage proposals. Let them know in advance and they can help with timing, location, and even photography coordination.

What about bad weather?

All charters offer full refunds or rescheduling for weather cancellations. Your captain will contact you if conditions are unsafe.

How far in advance should I book?

For private custom charters, 2-4 weeks ahead is recommended, especially during peak season (Dec-Apr). Larger groups (12+) should book earlier. Last-minute availability sometimes exists.

Are there other private charter operators to compare?
