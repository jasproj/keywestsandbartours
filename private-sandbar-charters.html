FAQ

Sandbar Trip Questions

What's the difference between per-person and whole-boat trips?

Per-person (shared) trips charge each guest a seat rate — from $125 per adult — and you share the boat. Whole-boat (private) charters are one flat rate for the boat, from $450 for up to 6. Small groups often pay less per-person; groups of four or more frequently come out ahead on a whole boat.

What's the cheapest way to a Key West sandbar?

A shared, per-person seat — currently from $125 per adult. With a group of four or more, splitting a whole-boat charter (from $450 for up to 6) can reach a similar or lower per-head cost.

What should we bring?

Sunscreen (reef-safe preferred), towels, food and drinks (BYOB is welcome on most charters), and a waterproof phone case. Most boats provide snorkel gear, coolers, and ice.

Will we see dolphins?

Often — dolphins are common in the backcountry, and a couple of these trips pair a sandbar stop with dolphin watching. Captains know where they gather but can't guarantee wild-animal sightings.
