The company

The islands most visitors only see from the highway

Up The Keys started in March 2016 as a family-run eco-tour outfit with an idea nobody else in Key West had turned into a business: put people in a van and drive them up the Lower Keys to the places a rental car and a map rarely find. Bahia Honda's beach, the Key Deer on Big Pine, the Blue Hole, the old and new 7 Mile Bridges, with park fees in the ticket.

Tours are capped at 12 and leave from wherever you are staying. Troy, the owner, leads most days; reviewers write about his commentary as often as the deer. Private day tours run the other direction too, meeting guests in Key Largo, Marathon or Miami and delivering them to Key West with stops on the way.

Before you go

Know before you go

Pickup

Free pickup and drop-off at any hotel, rental or address in Key West and Stock Island. Private day tours start in Marathon, Key Largo, Islamorada or Miami.

Included

Guide, transport, and park and refuge fees on the nature tour. Picnic lunch on the Dolphin Research Center tour.

Group size

Twelve maximum, two minimum on public tours. Private tours include four and add people at $35.

Kids

All ages on the nature and day tours, $35 for 12 and under. The tiki bar crawl is 21+.

Questions

Good to know

What is on the nature tour?

Big Pine Key for the Key deer and the Blue Hole, Bahia Honda State Park, and the 7 Mile Bridge, in four hours with pickup at your hotel. Park fees included.

Do you pick up at hotels?

Yes, anywhere in Key West or Stock Island, free.

How big are the groups?

Twelve at most in a 15-passenger van.

What does it cost?

The nature tour is $119 per adult and $35 for kids 12 and under. Day tours to the Dolphin Research Center or Aquarium Encounters are $159. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can you meet us up the Keys?

Yes. Private day tours start in Marathon ($549 for up to four), Key Largo or Islamorada ($679), or Miami, and end in Key West.
