The company

From a sailboat named Raven to a Belzona named Still Dreamin'

Capt. Zak cruised Florida for years on his sailboat Raven, explored the Everglades and the Lower Keys, and settled in Key West to start Casual Monday. The boat is Still Dreamin', a 32-foot Belzona luxury center console, with an Intrepid 262 as the second boat. Captains Nate Martin and TJ run trips alongside him.

Everything is private, six guests on the captained trips, and the itinerary is the group's: reef snorkeling, sandbar time, wildlife in the backcountry, or the sunset. The boat also books as a bareboat for up to twelve with your own licensed operator.
