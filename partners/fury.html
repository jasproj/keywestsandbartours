The company

Twelve catamarans and about two hundred people

Fury started in 1994. Scott Saunders, who had Fury as a client in the early nineties, bought it in 2004 when the previous owners asked, and grew it from a staff of 12 to around 200. The fleet as reported in 2019: twelve catamarans, eight parasail boats, nine rental boats, sixty jet skis and a glass-bottom boat. Fury builds its own cats, about fourteen months each.

It is a NOAA Blue Star snorkel operator and a founding member of Dolphin SMART, and has won the Keys' Bubba Award for best watersports company more than ten times. Reviews are what you would expect at this scale: crews get praised, add-on pricing and reef conditions get the complaints.

Snacks, fruit and veggies aboard, soft drinks, and beer and wine once the activities are done.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to board

Four docks; your confirmation names one. Opal Key at 241 Front St for snorkel, parasail and jet ski; the Historic Seaport at 631 Greene St for Ultimate Adventure, dolphin, sandbar and sunset trips; 0 Duval for the glass-bottom boat and jet skis; the Hyatt at 601 Front St for jet skis.

Check-in

30 minutes early; 60 minutes for Ultimate Adventure and jet skis. Photo ID and the booking card. Under-18s need a parent at check-in.

Included

Water, soda, beer and wine on most trips, alcohol after the activities. Snorkel gear and reef-safe sunscreen. You can bring food and non-alcoholic drinks in a soft cooler, no glass, no outside alcohol.

Parking

None provided. Street, pay lots and garages in Old Town.

Limits

Parasail minimum age 6, 450-lb limit. Jet ski operators 18+, or 14+ with a parent; 460 lb and three riders per ski.

Questions

Good to know

What is the Ultimate Adventure?

A 6.5-hour day on a 65-foot catamaran: reef snorkel, parasailing, jet skis, banana boat and an inflatable ocean water park, with lunch snacks and drinks. $143 per adult. Minimum age 4.

Where does Fury leave from?

Four docks in Old Town: Opal Key (241 Front St), the Historic Seaport (631 Greene St), 0 Duval Street, and the Hyatt Centric (601 Front St). Your confirmation says which.

Can we bring our own drinks?

Food and non-alcoholic drinks in a soft cooler, yes. No glass and no outside alcohol; beer and wine are served after the activities on most trips.

What does snorkeling cost?

The morning reef trip is $39 per adult, the afternoon Reef & Ritas $49, the Double Dip $63. Checked September 30, 2026.

Is Fury eco-certified?

NOAA Blue Star for snorkeling and a founding member of Dolphin SMART.
