The company

Family-operated, one boat, no crowds

Mermaid Charters is locally owned and family-operated around Capt. Mike, who has run boats in these waters for more than thirty years. The boat is a twin-engine 30-foot Scarab with a lily pad, two paddleboards, snorkel gear, a cooler and ice. Every trip is private for up to six.

The menu is longer than most one-boat outfits: eleven trips, including a four-hour lunch run to the Geiger Key Marina fish camp with a Boca Chica sandbar stop, a tiki-bar crawl by water, and a five-hour tiki, Snipes Point and sunset combination. Reviews describe Mike as laid-back and accommodating, the trips as built around the group.

The one nobody else runs

Lunch at the Geiger Key fish camp, by boat

Four hours: out through the backcountry to the Geiger Key Marina fish camp on the Atlantic side, tie up for lunch at the waterfront restaurant, then a stop at the Boca Chica sandbar on the way home. The boat brings the lily pad and paddleboards; lunch is on you.

The tiki-bar version does the same idea with waterfront bars instead of the fish camp, and there is a five-hour tiki, Snipes Point, sandbar and sunset day at $1,500.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to board

The site lists 241 Front Street in Old Town; other listings give North Roosevelt Boulevard. Your confirmation says where, and it is worth confirming by phone: (305) 735-4358.

Included

Captain, lily pad, two paddleboards, snorkel gear, ice, water and cooler space.

Bring

Your own food and drinks. Alcohol 21+, and not before snorkeling.

Cancellation

Free with seven days' notice, 50% at three to six days, full charge inside two days, per their policies page. A 3% card fee applies.

Questions

Good to know

How many people can go?

Six, all ages. Every trip is private and the price is for the boat.

What is the Geiger Key trip?

A four-hour run to the Geiger Key Marina fish camp for lunch at the waterfront restaurant, with a Boca Chica sandbar stop. $750 for the boat; lunch is on you.

Can we bring drinks?

Yes, BYOB. Alcohol is 21+ and not before snorkeling.

What does it cost?

From $500 for snorkeling or the champagne sunset to $1,500 for the five-hour tiki, Snipes, sandbar and sunset day. Checked September 30, 2026.

Where does the boat leave from?

Confirm with the booking; listings differ between Front Street and North Roosevelt.
