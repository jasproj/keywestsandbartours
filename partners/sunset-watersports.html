The company

One boat in 1984, five catamarans now

Richard C. Welter started Sunset Watersports in 1984 at 25 with one boat and helped bring parasailing to South Florida. His son Rich Welter is co-owner today, and the boats carry the family's names: Miss Lora, Sandie Cat, Miss Valyn. The Key West Chamber marked the 40th anniversary in 2024 and called it the largest family-founded and family-run watersports company in North America.

Four bases: the Historic Seaport at 201 William Street for the cruises, snorkel and sandbar trips; Parrot Key Resort for jet skis; Cow Key Marina on Stock Island for rentals and eco tours; and Smathers Beach for parasailing. Blue Star Operator, and a Plant a Million Corals partner.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to board

Cruises, snorkel, sandbar and dolphin trips: 201 William Street in the Historic Seaport. Jet skis: Parrot Key Resort, 2801 N Roosevelt Blvd, with a free shuttle. Parasailing: the seaport boat or Smathers Beach at 1910 S Roosevelt. Rentals and eco tours: Cow Key Marina, 5001 5th Street, Stock Island. Your confirmation says which.

Check-in

Do It All checks in at 9:15 am, 45 minutes before departure. Other trips list their own times on the booking page.

Included

Snorkel gear on reef trips. Lunch on the sandbar trips and Do It All. Open well bar on the Sip & Sail and the afternoon snorkel; unlimited draft beer after activities on Do It All.

Kids

Youth rate 5 to 12 (6 to 12 on Do It All), under 5 free. Boat rentals require a driver 25 or older.

Questions

Good to know

What is Do It All?

A six-hour day on the 65-foot Party Cat anchored at a floating playground: snorkeling, jet skis, kayaks, paddleboards, water trampoline, slide, climbing wall, banana boat and more, with grilled lunch. $139.95 per adult, $149.95 with a parasail flight.

Where do trips leave from?

Most from 201 William Street in the Historic Seaport. Jet skis from Parrot Key Resort, parasailing from the seaport or Smathers Beach, rentals from Cow Key Marina on Stock Island.

Is it family-owned?

Yes. Founded by Richard C. Welter in 1984; his son Rich Welter is co-owner.

What does snorkeling cost?

The Rise & Reef morning snorkel is $45.95 per adult; the afternoon Dip & Sip is $49.95. Checked September 30, 2026.

How much is the sunset cruise?

The Sip & Sail is $69.95 per adult with an open well bar, appetizers and live music. The dinner cruise price is on the booking page.
