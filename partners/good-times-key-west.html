The captains

A Michigan angler and an Army veteran

Justin Ferrell grew up fishing in Michigan, trained in marine propulsion, spent two years on high-performance engines on Lake Tahoe and moved to Florida in 2007. He started guiding in Key West in 2010 and runs Good Times with his family. Ryan Aper, the second captain, is a decorated Army veteran with a 100-ton master's license and a sailing endorsement, in Key West since 2011.

The trips are built around the group rather than a menu. Fishing licenses, tackle, live bait, snorkel gear, a cooler with water and ice, shade, a stereo and a propane grill are on the boat; bring your own food and drinks. Reviews call it the best charter in town more than once; Tripadvisor has it at 5.0 across 118 reviews and FishingBooker at 4.9 across 231.

The long one

Dry Tortugas on the Carolina Classic

Seventy miles west to Fort Jefferson on the 35-foot Carolina Classic, a boat with an air-conditioned cabin, galley, head and radar. The day trip is twelve hours from 8 am; the overnight is two days and one night with a captain and a first mate, sleeping aboard or camping on Garden Key with rented gear, up to six.

Fuel is billed separately on the Tortugas trips, and the park fee is not stated on the listing; ask when you book.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to board

The tiki hut at Mellow Ventures Cafe, 1605 N Roosevelt Boulevard. Free parking across the street at the Veterans Memorial flagpoles.

Included

Fishing licenses, rods and tackle, live bait, fish cleaning, snorkel masks and fins in limited sizes, a cooler with water and ice, shade, Bluetooth stereo and a propane grill.

Bring

Your own food and drinks. Life jackets are required for kids under six and provided.

Pricing

Sailfish prices include two guests and add up to six on the booking page. Carolina Classic prices include six. Dry Tortugas trips bill fuel separately.

Questions

Good to know

What can we do on a charter?

Anything the boat and the day allow: fishing, snorkeling, sandbars, island hopping, dolphins, sunset, or a mix. Tell them what the group wants.

How many people fit?

Six on either boat. The Sailfish price includes two and adds guests on the booking page; the Carolina Classic includes six.

Can we go to the Dry Tortugas?

Yes, on the 35-foot Carolina Classic: a 12-hour day trip for $2,000 or an overnight for $4,000, fuel extra.

Where does it leave from?

Mellow Ventures Cafe at 1605 N Roosevelt Boulevard, with free parking across the street.

What does it cost?

From $500 for two hours on the Sailfish to $1,750 for eight hours on the Carolina Classic. Checked September 30, 2026.
