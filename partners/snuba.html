The company

The in-between of snorkeling and scuba

SNUBA was invented in Hawaii: a scuba regulator on a hose that runs up to an air tank floating on a raft, so a first-timer can go to twenty feet after a short briefing, no certification, no heavy gear. SNUBA of Key West has been a licensed SNUBA center since 1999 and is the go-to for cruise-ship days and families with kids who are too young for a scuba course.

The boat is Good Times, a 47-foot catamaran deck boat built in 2012 and certified for 49, with a head, a shower and a wet bar; the same boat runs the Key West Cocktail Cruise at sunset. A 32-foot Century center console, Lori C, does the Looe Key private charters out of Big Pine Key. Guides are PADI, NAUI or SSI certified, and the company also runs full PADI courses.

The one to book

SNUBA: Go Beyond Snorkeling

Three to four hours: the ride out on Good Times, a fifteen-to-twenty-minute orientation, then a guided dive on the reef breathing from the surface line, with a snorkel session on the same site. All gear and instruction included, reverse-osmosis fresh water aboard to rinse.

Adults $120, kids 8 to 12 $100. Certified divers can bring their own tanks for $105, and snorkelers or riders come along for $65. The honest caveat from reviews: the SNUBA portion itself is about half an hour in the water.

Know before you go

Where to board

9 am and 1 pm for the daily reef trips. Sunset cruise times follow the sun.

All SNUBA and dive gear, instruction, fresh-water rinse. Cocktail cruise: unlimited drinks and light bites.

Questions

Good to know

What is SNUBA?

Diving with a regulator connected by a 20-foot hose to an air tank on a raft at the surface. No tank on your back, no certification; a short orientation and a guide in the water with you.

How old do you have to be?

Eight for SNUBA. Certified divers 15 and up for the Vandenberg wreck. The cocktail cruise is adults only.

Where does it leave from?

Garrison Bight Marina, 711 Eisenhower Drive, Slip 7, with free parking. Looe Key charters go from Big Pine Key.

What does it cost?

SNUBA $120 per adult, $100 for kids 8 to 12. Two-tank scuba dives $105. The cocktail cruise from $55. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can certified divers come?

Yes. Two-tank reef, wreck and Vandenberg dives at $105, and PADI courses.
