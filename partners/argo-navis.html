The company

Built for Cuba, kept for Key West

Three partners bought Argo Navis unfinished, meaning to run private charters to Cuba. When the rules on Cuba travel changed, she joined the Hindu Charters fleet in 2016 instead, and it took two years to finish her out for passengers. At 75 feet by 35, certified for 91, she is the largest sailing catamaran in Key West and the last commercial sailing vessel built on the island.

Hindu Charters is Capt. Josh Rowan, who started a charter business in Key West at 15, with Capt. Emyl Hattingh as part owner and primary skipper and Walter Shurtenburger, who designed the boat, as partner. The sister ships, the 1925 schooner Hindu and the cutter Bloodhound, summer in Provincetown.

The one to book

The all-inclusive sunset sail

Two hours from Sunset Pier. Premium wine, craft beer, handmade cocktails and soft drinks are included, and the crew passes six rounds of appetizers made aboard: caprese, charcuterie, smoked fish dip, dates with goat cheese. Blankets and pillows on deck, two restrooms below, no playlist.

One price for every age; kids ten and under wear a life jacket. Dogs welcome. Bring your own bottle only if it is something special and you have arranged it first; the bar is licensed.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to board

Sunset Pier at 0 Duval Street, behind Ocean Key Resort. Left side of the pier; look for the mast at the end of the dock. Arrive 15 minutes early; the boat leaves on time.

Parking

Paid lots at Mallory Square and the CVS garage. Rideshare is the easy answer at the foot of Duval.

Included

Premium wine, craft beer, handmade cocktails, soft drinks and six rounds of appetizers on the sunset sail; bottomless mimosas and bloody marys on brunch sails. Two restrooms aboard.

Kids and dogs

All ages, one price on the sunset sail. Ten and under wear a life jacket. Dog friendly.

Cancellation

48 hours by phone, per their site.

Questions

Good to know

Is Argo Navis a schooner?

No. She is a 75-foot sailing catamaran. The schooner in the same family is Hindu, which sails Provincetown in summer.

What does all-inclusive mean?

Premium wine, craft beer, handmade cocktails, soft drinks and six rounds of appetizers on the sunset sail. Brunch sails add bottomless mimosas and bloody marys.

Where does it leave from?

Sunset Pier at the foot of Duval Street, behind Ocean Key Resort.

What does it cost?

The sunset sail is $111 per person, all ages. The Sunday Sail is $109 adult, $85 child. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can kids come?

Yes, at the same price. Children ten and under wear a life jacket.
