Before you go

Questions

What is bully net fishing?

Bully netting is a technique for catching Florida spiny lobster at night. An underwater light draws the lobster out of hiding; a tickle stick coaxes it forward, and a bully net is dropped over it. No spear or hook is used. It is a legal recreational harvest method during the open Florida lobster season.

How many people does the boat take?

A maximum of 4 guests. The trip is private — your group has the whole boat to itself.

How long is the trip?

Three hours, at night.

What is the price?

$600 for the whole boat, regardless of group size (up to 4 guests). Checked against FareHarbor on 2026-10-22.

When is lobster season?

Florida’s regular recreational spiny lobster season runs August 6 through March 31. A two-day sport-diver mini-season takes place on the last consecutive Wednesday and Thursday before August 6. Bag limits and size rules are set by the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission — verify at myfwc.com before you book.

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes. Each guest who harvests lobster needs a current Florida saltwater fishing license with a lobster stamp. You can purchase one at GoOutdoorsFlorida.com before the trip. The captain’s license covers the vessel.
