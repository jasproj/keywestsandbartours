The crew

Captain Brad, Captain Maddie and two catamarans

Restless Native runs private charters on two sailing catamarans, the 42-foot ARIA and the 60-foot Zodiac. Every trip is one group, capped at six guests.

Captain Brad Barker runs the company. Guests name him and Captain Maddie in review after review; Maddie is also the cook aboard. The Restless Native name has been on Key West charter boats since 1993.

The big one

Three days to the Dry Tortugas

A private three-day trip from Key West to Fort Jefferson aboard the Zodiac. The boat has four cabins, each with its own bathroom and shower; three are for guests. The crew cooks every meal, and there is nothing for sale on the island.

Because you sleep aboard, you have the park in the early morning and evening, when the day boats are gone. Restless Native says it holds a permit to guide in Dry Tortugas National Park.

Whole boat, 3 days, up to 6$10,700

They recommend booking before you buy plane tickets; the trip often books up months ahead.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to board

Historic Seaport, 120 Margaret St. ARIA boards at Pier C-18 and the Zodiac at Pier D-22. They suggest parking in the lot next to Cuban Coffee Queen.

What to pack

Soft bags only; hard suitcases don't fit. It's a barefoot boat. Bring a swimsuit, sunscreen, a hat, and a windbreaker for winter sunsets.

Included

Snorkel gear, towels, inner tubes and pool floats.

Add-ons

Drinks and appetizers, a personal chef, Starlink Wi-Fi, an eFoil session and spearfishing.

One house rule

No red wine aboard. It spills, and mixed with salt water it stains like paint.

Questions

Good to know

How many guests can come?

Up to six per charter on either boat. Every trip is private.

Where do we board?

At the Historic Seaport, 120 Margaret St, Key West. ARIA is at Pier C-18 and the Zodiac at Pier D-22.

Is food included?

On day sails you can bring your own or add drinks and appetizers or a personal chef. On the Dry Tortugas trip the crew provides the meals.

Can we stay overnight?

Yes. The Overnight Expedition on the Zodiac is $3,700, and the Dry Tortugas trip is three days aboard for $10,700.

What is the difference between ARIA and the Zodiac?

ARIA is a 42-foot catamaran and the Zodiac is a 60-foot Fountaine Pajot. Both take up to six guests; the Zodiac costs $1,000 more on day sails.
