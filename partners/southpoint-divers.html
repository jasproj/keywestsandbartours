The company

Thirty-five years on Front Street

Southpoint Divers opened in 1991. Don McKenzie moved to Key West in 1994, got certified, worked at the shop through the mid-nineties and bought it before the decade was out; he is a PADI instructor and a USCG master, and runs it with Patti. The shop is at 606 Front Street with a full ScubaPro retail floor, and the boat, M/V Phoenix, a 46-foot Newton, ties up at the Hyatt marina across the street.

The schedule has not changed in years: the Vandenberg at 8, the reef at 1, sunset and night dives Wednesdays and Saturdays. Southpoint is PADI 5-Star, a NOAA Blue Star operator, a ScubaPro Platinum dealer, and the Coral Restoration Foundation's public dive partner in Key West, which means coral nursery and out-planting dives on the calendar.

The one to book

The Vandenberg, every morning

The USNS General Hoyt S. Vandenberg is a 523-foot ship sunk seven miles off Key West in 2009, the second-largest artificial reef in the world. Southpoint dives it every morning at 8 as a two-tank trip. Certified divers 15 and up, dove within the past year, and a guide is required without an Advanced Open Water card.

With a gear package it is $174; with your own gear, tanks and weights only, $134. Riders who want to see the boat come along for $65. The afternoon reef trip is the same price and takes snorkelers at $70.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

The shop at 606 Front Street: 8 am for the wreck, 1 pm for the reef, 12:30 for coral-restoration dives. The boat is at the Hyatt marina across the street.

Vandenberg rules

Certified, 15 and up, a logged dive within the past year, and a guide unless you hold Advanced Open Water.

Included

Full gear rental packages, a restroom aboard, dive lights on night dives.

Beginners

Discover Scuba takes non-certified swimmers from 10 through a pool session and two reef dives in one day.

Parking

Downtown garages and lots; none at the shop.

Questions

Good to know

Can beginners dive?

Yes. Discover Scuba is a one-day program for swimmers 10 and up: pool in the morning, two reef dives in the afternoon, $259, no certification needed.

What is the Vandenberg?

A 523-foot ship sunk in 2009 as an artificial reef, seven miles off Key West. Southpoint dives it every morning; certified divers 15 and up with a dive in the past year.

Where is the shop?

606 Front Street in Old Town. The boat is at the Hyatt marina across the street.

What does it cost?

Two-tank dives $174 with gear or $134 with your own, Discover Scuba $259, Open Water certification $549. Checked September 30, 2026.
