The company

Named after a boat, not a risk

The first schooner was called Danger, and renaming a boat is bad luck, so the company kept the name when it grew. It has been locally owned since 1993 and sails 364 days a year from the Opal Key marina at the foot of Front Street, with a second base at the Perry on Stock Island.

The fleet is four wooden schooners, each USCG-certified, plus a six-person center-console powerboat. Day trips cap at 20 guests even though the boats carry more, which is the reason the sail-snorkel-kayak day feels semi-private.

The signature day

Sail, snorkel and kayak in the wildlife refuge

The schooner sails west into the Key West National Wildlife Refuge, anchors on the flats, and the kayaks come off the deck for a guided paddle through the mangroves. Then a snorkel stop, then the sail home. Wine, beer, soft drinks and snacks are on board; the premium 6.5-hour version adds a buffet lunch.

All ages, masks and fins provided, wetsuits in winter. Twenty guests maximum on a boat certified for more.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to board

Key West trips check in at the Danger Chandlery store at Opal Key Resort & Marina, 255 Front Street, 30 minutes before departure. Stock Island trips leave from the Perry Hotel & Marina, 7001 Shrimp Road, J Dock, Slip 5, with free parking in the second lot past the hotel.

Parking downtown

Meters on the block or the underground garage, which is often full. Walking, biking or a taxi is the easier answer at the Front Street end.

Included

Snorkel gear, kayaks, life jackets, wine, beer, soft drinks and snacks on day trips; lunch on the premium day. Wetsuits in winter.

Cancellation

24 hours for groups of four or fewer, 72 hours for five or more, 15 days for private charters, per their FAQ.

Questions

Good to know

Where does Danger Charters leave from?

Most trips leave from Opal Key Resort & Marina at 255 Front Street in Key West. Some sails run from the Perry Hotel & Marina on Stock Island; your confirmation says which.

How many people are on the boat?

Public day trips are capped at 20 guests even though the schooners are certified for 26 to 30. Private charters take up to 22 to 30 depending on the boat.

Is the sunset sail kids-friendly?

The public Wind & Wine sail is 13 and up. The day trips and the private sunset sail are all ages.

What does it cost?

Day trips from $105 per adult, the sunset sail $115, private powerboat trips from $399, private schooner charters from $1,600. Checked September 30, 2026.

Do I need to bring snorkel gear?

No. Masks, fins, snorkels and kayaks are provided, and wetsuits in winter.
