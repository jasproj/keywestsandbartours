The captain

Sixteen years on this water, first as a commercial fisherman

Capt. Sergey Chashchukhin fished commercially out of Key West from 2008 before switching to charters full time. It is an owner-operator outfit: he runs every trip himself, so the person who answers the phone is the one driving the boat.

The boat is a Sea Born LX 26 center console with twin 200-horsepower Suzukis, fast enough to reach the backcountry or the reef with time to spare. Trips are priced for the whole boat and the itinerary bends to the weather and the group.

The one to book

Four hours of sandbar, on your terms

The four-hour sandbar trip is the flexible one. Start at a sandbar, add a snorkel stop or a run through the mangroves for dolphins, and come back when the group is done. Snorkel gear and drinks are aboard; bring your own food and drinks, no glass.

Six-hour versions of the sandbar, snorkel and eco trips run $899, and the two-hour sandbar or sunset-and-dolphin trips are the cheapest private boat on this site at $350.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to board

Hurricane Hole Marina, 5130 Overseas Highway #1, Stock Island. Free marina parking. Your confirmation has the dock and time.

Bring

Your own food and drinks, no glass. Snorkel gear, drinks, wakeboard, tube and fishing tackle are aboard depending on the trip.

Fishing

Florida license, rods, reels, tackle, bait and fish cleaning are included on fishing trips.

Kids and dogs

All ages, and the boat is pet-friendly.

Questions

Good to know

How many people can go?

Six, all ages. Every trip is private; the price is for the boat.

Where does it leave from?

Hurricane Hole Marina on Stock Island, 5130 Overseas Highway, about ten minutes from Old Town by car. Free parking.

Can we bring our own drinks?

Yes, including alcohol. No glass aboard.

What does it cost?

Two-hour trips $350, four-hour trips $699 to $749, six-hour trips $899 to $949 for the whole boat. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can we mix activities?

Yes. The four- and six-hour trips are built around whatever the group wants: sandbar, snorkeling, mangroves, dolphins, tubing.
