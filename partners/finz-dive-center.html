The company

Small-island diving in Key West

Robert Trosset III started Finz in 2011 because Key West diving meant big boats and fixed schedules, and he wanted the small-boat, personal experience of diving on a small island. Rives Millwater, a captain with 25 years of diving out of the Outer Banks, is co-owner. The boats are six-packs, every trip carries a divemaster who gets in the water, and the team page names every captain and instructor.

Two boats: Finz Freeman and Park Shark, a 35-foot Razorhead with a Dometic gyro stabilizer, an air-conditioned cabin, a full head and a bow ramp for landing on Loggerhead Key in the Tortugas. Finz is a NOAA Blue Star operator and a Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice 2026 pick, ranked #18 of 397 in Key West.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

6003 Peninsular Avenue, Suite 4, Stock Island; the boats are at the marina there. Your confirmation has the dock and time. Dives leave at 8 am and 1 pm.

Certification

Public dives are for certified divers; bring your card at booking and expect a medical questionnaire. Snorkelers can ride the reef trip.

Included

A divemaster in the water on every dive. Dry Tortugas trips include lunch, snacks and water. Gear rental is extra.

Fuel

A fuel surcharge applies when fuel is over $5 a gallon, per their FAQ.

Catch

Spearfishing trips include fish cleaning, and reviews mention shipping the catch home.

Questions

Good to know

How many divers per boat?

Six. Both boats are six-packs, and a divemaster is in the water on every dive.

Do I need to be certified?

For the dives, yes; bring your card. Snorkelers can join the reef trip at $175.

Where does Finz leave from?

Stock Island, at 6003 Peninsular Avenue, about ten minutes from Old Town.

What does it cost?

Two-tank dives $199, private charters from $1,050 plus tanks per diver, Dry Tortugas from $839 a seat or $2,999 private. Checked September 30, 2026.

What is Park Shark?

A 35-foot Razorhead with a gyro stabilizer, an air-conditioned cabin and a bow ramp, used for the Dry Tortugas and long runs.
