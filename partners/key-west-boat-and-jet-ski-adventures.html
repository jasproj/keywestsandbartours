The company

Smaller groups on newer skis

The pitch is in the name of their About page: smaller groups, better experience. Guided jet ski tours go out with a maximum of eight skis where the big operators run fifteen to twenty, on Sea-Doo GTX 130 Pros that are turned over every year through a BRP partnership. Two riders per ski, 400-pound limit, ages 4 and up as a passenger.

The boat side runs from the same Stock Island base on Cow Key Channel: a captained Manitou tri-toon for up to ten with a bimini, a lily mat, snorkel gear, a cooler with water and ice, speakers, and your own food and drinks welcome. The clear-kayak night paddle, LED-lit, is the one nobody else runs on this side of the island.

The one to book

The 90-minute jet ski tour

Ninety minutes around the island on a Sea-Doo with a guide and at most seven other skis. Life jacket, whistle and dry bag included, wetsuits on request. The loop depends on the weather and the group; reviews note it gets shortened when conditions or skill levels call for it.

The price is per ski, up to two riders. Drivers 18 and up rent; 16 and 17 can drive with an adult signing, 14 and 15 with an adult aboard. Florida boater card needed if born in 1988 or later.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

5016 5th Avenue, Stock Island, on Cow Key Channel. Arrive 30 minutes early with a government photo ID. Free parking on the night kayak listing; ask about the rest.

Jet ski rules

Two riders per ski, 400 pounds combined. Rent at 18; drive at 16 with an adult signing; drive at 14 with an adult aboard. Born 1988 or later needs a Florida boater card, which you can do online.

Boat charters

Captain, bimini, lily mat, snorkel gear, cooler with water and ice, speakers. Bring your own food and drinks.

Weather

Full refund on weather cancellations; otherwise no refund inside 24 hours, per their site.

Questions

Good to know

How many jet skis go out at once?

Eight at most, with a guide. Most Key West operators run fifteen to twenty.

Can kids ride?

As passengers from age 4. Drivers are 18 and up, or 16 with an adult signing, or 14 with an adult aboard.

Where is it?

5016 5th Avenue on Stock Island, on Cow Key Channel, about ten minutes from Old Town.

What does it cost?

Jet ski tours from $139.95 per ski for two riders. Boat charters $449.95 for four hours, up to ten. The night clear-kayak paddle $79.95. Checked September 30, 2026.

What is the Luminous Lagoon?

A guided 90-minute paddle after dark in clear kayaks lit with LEDs, through the mangroves off Stock Island.
