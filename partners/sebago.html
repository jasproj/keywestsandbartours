The company

Started with a racing catamaran in 1988

Paul McGrail, a 200-ton master, and boat builder Sean Rowley bought the 50-foot racing catamaran Sebago, a boat sponsored by the shoe company and raced by Walter Greene, and started running day charters from the Key West seaport in 1988. The company calls itself the longest-established day-charter catamaran operation in the continental US.

Since 2019 Sebago has been owned by RED Hospitality & Leisure, part of Ashford Inc. The fleet is four big catamarans, a tiki cat, a 37-foot Axopar for private trips and two parasail boats, with a yellow office on the Harbor Walk between Schooner Wharf Bar and Conch Republic Seafood.

The one to book

Mimosa morning snorkel

The morning trip to the reef with breakfast and bottomless mimosas aboard. Mask, fins and vest are provided; the reef is seven miles out and the catamarans are big enough to keep the ride comfortable. Kids 4 to 11 pay the child rate; under 4 free.

The afternoon snorkel is the cheaper seat at $44.95, and the Reef to Table sail adds a seafood lunch to the snorkel and a dolphin watch.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

The yellow office at 205 Elizabeth Street, Unit 1, on the Harbor Walk between Schooner Wharf Bar and Conch Republic Seafood, reached by Lazy Way Lane. Your confirmation names the dock.

Parking

None at the office. Drop passengers out front and park at the Greene Street lot or the city lot at Margaret and Caroline.

Included

Snorkel gear (mask, fins, vest) on reef trips; food and drinks vary by trip and are listed on each. Full bars on the big cats, 21+ with ID.

Kids

Under 18 with a parent or guardian. Child rate 4 to 11 on most trips, under 4 free. Guests with allergies are asked to bring their own food.

Questions

Good to know

Where does Sebago leave from?

The Historic Seaport. Check in at the yellow office at 205 Elizabeth Street on the Harbor Walk; the boats are on the docks behind it.

How far is the reef?

About seven miles out. The catamarans make the ride comfortable in most conditions.

Is gear included?

Yes. Mask, fins and vest on every snorkel trip.

What does it cost?

Snorkel trips $44.95 to $69.95 per adult, sunset sails $49.95 to $79.95, the Sandbar Safari $129.95. Checked September 30, 2026.

Who owns Sebago?

RED Hospitality & Leisure, part of Ashford Inc., since 2019. It was founded by Paul McGrail and Sean Rowley in 1988.
