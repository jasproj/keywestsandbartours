The captain

Mr. Nice Guy

Jeffrey Lapierre started fishing commercially out of Massachusetts as a way through college, bluefin tuna and lobster, and came to the Keys in the 2000s for water that fishes all year. He has captained for more than twenty years and set up Nice Guy Charters in 2018. Reviews are about patience: with kids, with first-timers, with a group that wants to fish for two hours and float for two.

The boat is a 24-foot Everglades center console with a 300-horsepower Suzuki, a livewell and a trolling motor, out of Cow Key Marina on Stock Island. Rods, tackle, live bait, licenses, fillet service and cold drinks are included on fishing trips; bring sandwiches.

Where to board

Questions

Good to know

How many people can go?

Six. Fishing prices cover five and add $75 for the sixth; tours take six at the listed price.

Where does it leave from?

Cow Key Marina, 5001 5th Avenue on Stock Island, about ten minutes from Old Town.

What is included on fishing trips?

Rods, tackle, live bait, the Florida license, fish cleaning and cold drinks. Bring lunch.

What does it cost?

Fishing from $600 for three hours to $1,100 for eight. Sandbar $650, sunset $450, snorkeling $600. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can we combine fishing with something else?

Yes. The combination trip pairs a fishing charter with a sandbar, snorkel or dolphin tour; call to set it up.
