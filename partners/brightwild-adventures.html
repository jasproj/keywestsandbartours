The company

The boat arm of a Key West hospitality brand

Brightwild is a Key West vacation-rental and hospitality company founded by Billy Spottswood; Brightwild Adventures is its boat operation, running since 2023 from the Marriott Beachside on North Roosevelt. The boat is a custom 53-foot pontoon certified for 35, with a sister ship that brings private-charter capacity to 70.

Unlike most sandbar boats, the crew is named on the site: three captains and three first mates. The format is all-inclusive, with a sandwich lunch, water and alcohol on the sandbar trips and appetizers, drinks and a champagne toast on the sunset cruise, and no outside food or drink.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to board

The beach behind the Marriott Beachside, 3841 N Roosevelt Boulevard. Park in the hotel garage next to the lobby. Bring a photo ID.

Included

Kayaks, paddleboards, lily pad and floats; sandwich lunch, water and alcohol on the sandbar trips; appetizers, drinks and a champagne toast on the sunset cruise.

Outside food and drink

Not allowed; everything is aboard.

Ages

Family sandbar and sunset are all ages with kids supervised; the adults-only sandbar is 18 and up.

Cancellation

72 hours, per their FAQ.

Questions

Good to know

Where does it leave from?

The beach behind the Marriott Beachside at 3841 N Roosevelt Boulevard, with parking in the hotel garage.

Is lunch included?

Yes, a sandwich lunch plus water and alcohol on the sandbar trips. The sunset cruise includes appetizers, drinks and a champagne toast. No outside food or drink.

Is there an adults-only option?

Yes, the noon sandbar is 18 and up. The morning sandbar and the sunset cruise are all ages.

What does it cost?

Sandbar trips $124.99 to $134.99 per adult, the sunset cruise $79.95. Checked September 30, 2026.

Can we book the whole boat?

Yes, up to 40 on one boat or 70 across two. The private two-hour sunset is $2,500; longer charters are quoted on the booking page.
