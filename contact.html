Need help picking the right tour for your group, budget, or dates? Send us the basics and we'll point you toward the right local operators.

Before you reach out

  • For booking changes or cancellations: contact the operator directly. Their info is on every tour page and your confirmation email.
  • For tour recommendations based on your group, budget, or dates: use the form below.
  • For business inquiries (operators wanting to list, sponsors, partnerships, media): also use the form below.

What we can help with

  • Matching the right boat to your group size (families, couples, bachelor parties, big crews)
  • Finding family-friendly trips with shade and restrooms for young kids
  • Sandbar vs sunset vs snorkel — which one fits your day
  • Accessibility, wheelchair space, or pet policy questions on private charters
  • Group bookings, weddings, bachelor and bachelorette logistics
  • Sorting half-day vs full-day vs custom-route charters

We know the difference between a calm sandbar trip and a party boat. Tell us what you want and we'll point you to the right operators.
