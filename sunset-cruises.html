FAQ

Sunset Cruise Questions

What time do Key West sunset cruises leave?

Departure moves with the sunset: roughly 5:00 PM in December and 7:30 PM in June, usually 60 to 90 minutes before the sun goes down. The exact time is on the operator's calendar when you check availability.

Is alcohol included?

On most shared sails, yes — champagne, beer, wine and soft drinks are part of the ticket, and a few include a full open bar. Private charters are usually BYOB or include a welcome bottle; the card says which.

Shared sail or private boat?

Shared sails run $49.95 to $108 per person and are the right call for two people or a small group who want it easy. A private boat starts around $350 for up to six, so it is cheaper than a shared sail once you have five or more — and you control the music, the route and who is aboard.

What if the weather turns?

Captains call it, not you. If the operator cancels for weather you get a full refund or a rebook. Light rain rarely cancels; lightning and high wind do. Book for an early night of your trip so you have a backup evening.

Where do the boats leave from?

Almost all depart from the Historic Seaport (Key West Bight) or the Margaritaville Resort marina, both walkable from Duval Street. Parking near the seaport is tight — walk, bike or take a cab.
