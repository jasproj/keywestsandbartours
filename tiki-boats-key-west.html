FAQ

Key West Tiki Boat Questions

How much does a tiki boat cost in Key West?

Three tiers: a per-person seat on a shared cruise runs about $60–$80 per adult; a private boat for up to 6 (bachelorette/birthday) runs about $300–$525 for the whole boat; a big-group or longer charter, up to 8–12, runs about $1,049–$1,500. For 4+ people, a private whole-boat charter usually beats buying individual seats on cost per head.

What is included in a tiki boat rental?

A licensed captain, a cooler with ice, a Bluetooth speaker, and life jackets — plus float mats on most sandbar trips. Drinks and food are usually not included (most boats are BYOB). Captain gratuity (15–20%) is customary and not in the base price.

Can you drink on a tiki boat in Key West?

Yes. Florida law allows passengers to drink on a chartered vessel as long as the captain is sober and licensed. Most tiki boats are BYOB — no glass containers (cans and plastic only), don't board visibly intoxicated, and drink responsibly.

How many people fit on a tiki boat?

Most hold 6 to 12, set by Coast Guard certification. Common capacities: 6 (couples and small groups), 8 (bachelorette/birthday), and 12 (larger party charters). For groups over 12, book two boats.

What is a BYOB tiki boat?

BYOB means Bring Your Own Beverage. The operator provides the captain, boat, speakers, and a cooler with ice; you bring your own drinks — beer, wine, seltzers, or pre-mixed cocktails. No glass; cans and plastic only. BYOB is the default for Key West tiki boats because it lets your group bring exactly what it wants.

Are tiki boats good for a bachelorette party?

Yes — a private tiki boat is one of the most popular bachelorette activities in Key West. Book a private whole-boat charter (up to 6, or up to 8–12 for bigger groups), BYOB, bring decorations and a playlist, and add a sandbar stop. Private for up to 6 runs about $300–$525.

How do I book a Key West tiki boat?

Pick a tour above and tap Check Availability — it opens the operator's live FareHarbor calendar, where you choose a date and party size and book directly with the operator. Weekends, sunset slots, and bachelorette season fill early, so reserve ahead.
