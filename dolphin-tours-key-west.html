Frequently Asked Questions

Are dolphin sightings guaranteed?
No. These are wild dolphins, so sightings are not guaranteed — though they are highly likely on reputable tours, and captains know where pods usually gather. Cancellation and refund terms are set by the operator you book with, so check them at booking.
How close will we see dolphins?
Our eco-certified approach keeps dolphins safe. You'll see them at 50-100+ feet, close enough for amazing photos and video.
Best time of year?
Year-round operations. Spring and summer have higher activity. Tours run rain or shine—bring a light jacket in winter.
Can children join?
Absolutely! Kids 5+ love the tour. We have life jackets for all sizes. Families get group booking discounts.
