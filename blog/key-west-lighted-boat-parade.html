Key West Lighted Boat Parade: Viewing Guide, 2026 Date and How to Watch from the Water

Every mid-December the Key West Historic Seaport transforms for a night. Vessels of every size — decorated kayaks, fishing boats strung with LED nets, tall ships with lit rigging, pontoon barges with full light shows — parade through the harbor after dark while a crowd lines the seawall. The Lighted Boat Parade is one of the few events in Key West that is genuinely local in character: it is not aimed at tourists, it has been running for decades, and the boats competing for the lighting prize tend to be seriously committed.

You can watch for free from land. But the best view of a boat parade is from a boat — and there are two ways to get on the water for it.

Short answer: The 2026 Lighted Boat Parade is traditionally held on the second or third Saturday of December; the official date had not been confirmed at the time of writing. If the parade is your reason for visiting, book a viewing boat first — they sell out — then lock in flights and lodging.

What the parade looks like

The parade route runs through the basin of the Historic Seaport, roughly from the northwest end of the harbor past Schooner Wharf Bar and back. Participating vessels are decorated with lights — some tastefully, many extravagantly — and prizes are awarded in categories like best theme, most creative, and best overall.

The variety is what makes it worth watching. In a typical year you will see:

  • Sailboats with every inch of rigging outlined in white LEDs
  • Fishing charter boats with animated light displays on the hull
  • Small inflatables with a single string of battery-powered lights (the underdogs)
  • At least one boat that looks like a floating Christmas tree
  • The occasional tall ship, if a schooner operator enters

The parade takes about 60–90 minutes. Boats circle the harbor so the entire fleet passes the viewing area more than once.

2026 date: what we know

The Lighted Boat Parade is traditionally held on the second or third Saturday of December. In most recent years that has placed it somewhere between December 12 and December 20. The 2026 date had not been officially confirmed at the time this was written.

The most reliable way to track the confirmed date: check the booking pages for the on-water viewing boats (linked below). When Sebago and Honest Eco open tickets, the date is confirmed. Those pages update before most local event calendars do.

Land viewing: the free option

The seawall along the Historic Seaport waterfront is the best free viewing location. The stretch from the end of Margaret Street past Schooner Wharf Bar gives a clear sightline into the parade route. Schooner Wharf Bar itself stays open during the parade and is a reasonable base — outdoor bar, live music, and a direct view of the harbor.

Tips for land viewing

  • Arrive 60–90 minutes early. The seawall fills up. Spots with unobstructed sightlines are gone 45 minutes before the parade starts.
  • Bring something to stand on if you have small children. The seawall is low and adults block the view quickly.
  • Parking: The Historic Seaport area has limited parking. The Conch Train Lot on North Roosevelt is the closest public lot. Expect to walk 10–15 minutes. Rideshare and bicycle are easier.
  • Weather: December nights in Key West can be breezy. 60–68°F after dark with a 15-knot wind feels cooler than the daytime temperature suggests — bring a light layer.

On-water viewing: the better option

Watching from a boat in the harbor puts you inside the parade instead of at the edge of it. The procession moves through the water around you, not past you. The light reflections on the water are dramatically better from the surface than from the seawall. And you are not fighting a crowd.

There are two ways to get on the water for the parade: a shared catamaran tour, and a private boat charter.

The sunset before the parade

The parade is an evening event, but the day leading into it is worth planning. December sunsets in Key West happen around 5:40–5:45 p.m., and a sunset sail in the afternoon leaves you back at the seaport dock with an hour or two before the parade begins. The Champagne Sunset Sail on Schooner America 2.0 departs from the same Historic Seaport docks and returns well before dark.

Kids and the Lighted Boat Parade

The land-viewing option is excellent for families. The seawall is at street level, the event is free, and the boats are visually compelling for children of all ages. The parade moves slowly enough that kids can follow what is happening, and there is no admission gate or ticketed zone to manage.

On-water viewing with kids works best on the private Java Cat (up to 6), where you can move around and there are no strangers nearby. The shared catamaran is also kid-friendly for children old enough to be comfortable on a moving vessel for two hours at night without needing to sit in a specific spot.

The Cocoa and Carols cruise is a separate event — a daytime evening sail on the schooner — but if you are in Key West for the parade weekend, running the sunset sail on Friday and the parade on Saturday is a reasonable two-night combination. Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Key West Lighted Boat Parade 2026?

The Lighted Boat Parade is traditionally held on the second or third Saturday of December at the Key West Historic Seaport. The 2026 date had not been officially confirmed at the time of writing. The operator booking pages for the viewing-boat tours are the most reliable source for the confirmed date.

What time does the Key West Lighted Boat Parade start?

The parade typically begins after dark, around 6–7 p.m. Viewing-boat charters depart beforehand — usually around 5–6 p.m. — to position in the harbor before the parade starts. Arrive at the seaport at least 30 minutes before your boat's departure.

Is the Key West Lighted Boat Parade free to watch from land?

Yes. Viewing from the seawall, Schooner Wharf Bar, and the waterfront promenade at the Historic Seaport is free. Good spots fill up about an hour before the parade starts. On-water viewing-boat tickets are purchased separately.

Is the Lighted Boat Parade good for kids?

Yes, the land-viewing option from the seawall is very family-friendly. The on-water boats are also kid-friendly for children old enough to stay on a moving vessel for two hours at night. The private Java Cat option (up to 6 guests) gives families more flexibility than the shared catamaran.

How do I get tickets for the Lighted Boat Parade viewing boats?

Book directly through the operator's FareHarbor booking page. Sebago Watersports runs a shared catamaran for $89.95 per person. Honest Eco offers a private boat (Java Cat) for $799 for up to 6 guests. Both sell out before the event — book as soon as the date is confirmed.
