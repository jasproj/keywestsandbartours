Key West New Year's Eve: The Three Drops, Sunset Sails and December on the Water

Key West does New Year's Eve with three midnight drops instead of one ball, and every one of them is within a fifteen-minute walk of the others. The evening before midnight is the part most visitors leave to chance. It shouldn't be: December 31 has a 5:45 p.m. sunset, and the sunset sails that night are the best seats on the island until the drops start.

This is the plan we'd run: which drop to pick, how to get on the water first, and the December boat events that lead up to it.

Short answer: Sunset sail at 4:30–5 p.m. from the Historic Seaport, dinner in Old Town by 8, then pick one drop and be standing there by 10. The Pirate Wench at Schooner Wharf is the easiest to reach from the boats and the least crushed; the Red Shoe on upper Duval is the biggest show; the Conch Shell at Sloppy Joe's is the classic and the most crowded.

The three midnight drops

Red high-heel shoe — Bourbon St. Pub, 724 Duval

A giant red sequined shoe with a drag performer inside is lowered from the balcony at midnight. It is the most photographed of the three and the busiest stretch of Duval. Arrive two to three hours early for a sightline.

Conch shell — Sloppy Joe's, Duval and Greene

An illuminated six-foot conch shell drops from the roof of Sloppy Joe's. This is the block-party version: the corner is closed to traffic, the bar spills onto the street, and the crowd is the thickest on the island.

Pirate wench — Schooner Wharf Bar, Historic Seaport

A costumed pirate wench descends from the mast of the schooner America 2.0 with a cannon countdown. Waterfront, live music all evening, and a slightly older crowd. It is also the closest drop to where the sunset sails tie up, which makes it the natural end to a boat-first evening.

New Year's Eve sunset sails

Departures on December 31 cluster around 4:30–5 p.m. for a 5:45 sunset. Operators run their regular sunset trips that night; a few add a champagne pour. Book as early as you can — the seaport boats fill weeks out over the holidays.

Practical notes

  • Pick one drop. They are a 15–20 minute walk apart, but the crowds between them after 11 p.m. make hopping unrealistic. Choose by vibe: the shoe for spectacle, the shell for tradition, the wench for the waterfront.
  • Boats first, then dinner, then the drop. A 5 p.m. sunset sail lands you at the seaport by 7 with the whole evening ahead.
  • Wear shoes you can stand in for three hours. Every guide says it because it's true.
  • Rooms: New Year's week is the most expensive week of the year and sells out months ahead. The boats are usually the last thing people book and the first thing that's gone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the three New Year's Eve drops in Key West?

At midnight Key West lowers a giant red high-heeled shoe with a drag performer inside at Bourbon St. Pub on Duval Street, an illuminated conch shell from the roof of Sloppy Joe's at Duval and Greene, and a pirate wench from the mast of the schooner America 2.0 at Schooner Wharf Bar in the Historic Seaport.

What time is sunset in Key West on New Year's Eve?

About 5:45 p.m. Sunset sails on December 31 depart around 4:30–5 p.m. and return by roughly 7 p.m., which leaves the evening free for dinner and the midnight drops.

Are there boat tours on New Year's Eve in Key West?

Yes. Operators run their regular sunset sails on December 31, several with champagne, and private charters are available for groups. They sell out weeks ahead over the holidays.

When is the Key West Lighted Boat Parade?

The Lighted Boat Parade is held in mid-December at the Historic Seaport; the exact date is set each year and confirmed by the operators when viewing-boat tickets go on sale. Shared and private viewing boats run in the harbor during the parade.

Which New Year's Eve drop is least crowded?

The Pirate Wench lowering at Schooner Wharf Bar is the most spread out, on the waterfront with live music. The Conch Shell drop at Sloppy Joe's is the most crowded; the Red Shoe drop on upper Duval is in between and has the biggest show.
