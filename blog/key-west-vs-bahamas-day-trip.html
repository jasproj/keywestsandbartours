Key West to the Bahamas: No Ferry, So Do This Instead

No ferry, cruise ship or direct flight connects Key West to the Bahamas. The clear-water day trip that actually exists is the Dry Tortugas: 70 miles west, no passport, back by dinner.

Short answer: You can't do the Bahamas as a day trip from Key West. You'd first travel to Fort Lauderdale or Miami, which uses most of the day before you board anything bound for the islands. Plan the Bahamas as its own overnight trip. If what you want is clear water and a remote island in one day, book the Dry Tortugas instead.

In Key West October 16–25? That’s Fantasy Fest, with the parade on Saturday, October 24. Our Fantasy Fest boat guide covers sunset sails from $59.95 a person and private sandbar charters from $500, with open dates that week.

The route

How people actually get from Key West to the Bahamas

Fly

Key West to Fort Lauderdale or Miami is about an hour in the air. Flights from there to Nassau or Bimini take roughly another 40 to 90 minutes, plus the connection.

Ferry

Passenger ferries to Bimini and Grand Bahama leave from Fort Lauderdale, not Key West. Each way takes two to three hours.

Drive, then ferry

The Overseas Highway to Fort Lauderdale is a long drive on its own. Add a ferry and there is no time left for the islands the same day.

Documents

The Bahamas is an international trip, so bring the travel documents your airline or ferry asks for. Nothing in the Florida Keys, including the Dry Tortugas, needs a passport.

The day trip you're looking for

Dry Tortugas: Bahamas-style water, no passport

Dry Tortugas National Park sits about 70 miles west of Key West: shallow reef, sand, and Fort Jefferson on Garden Key. It's still the United States, so there's no customs line. Prices and open dates checked against FareHarbor on September 29, 2026.

Deciding

If you're choosing where to spend the trip

Stay in the Keys

if you want to drive in, skip the passport, and fill your days with sandbars, reef snorkeling, sunset sails and Duval Street at night.

Plan a separate Bahamas trip

if an international island is the point. Fly or take the ferry from South Florida and give it a few nights. Squeezing it into a Key West vacation mostly buys you time in airports.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you do a Bahamas day trip from Key West?

No. No ferry, cruise ship or direct flight connects Key West to the Bahamas. You would have to travel to Fort Lauderdale or Miami first, then fly or take a ferry, which leaves no time on the islands in the same day.

Is there a ferry from Key West to the Bahamas?

No. Ferries to Bimini and Grand Bahama leave from Fort Lauderdale and take two to three hours each way. The ferry that runs from Key West goes to Fort Myers Beach, not the Bahamas.

What's the closest thing to Bahamas water from Key West?

The Dry Tortugas. The national park is about 70 miles west of Key West, reachable in one day by boat or seaplane, with shallow reef snorkeling around Fort Jefferson. No passport is needed.

Do I need a passport?

For the Bahamas, bring the international travel documents your airline or ferry requires. For anything in the Florida Keys, including the Dry Tortugas, you don't need a passport.

How much does a Dry Tortugas day trip cost from Key West?

Private and small-boat charters listed here run $750 to $839 per person for a 10-hour day, snorkel gear and park entry included on the Finz trip. The national park ferry and seaplane are covered on our Dry Tortugas day trip page.
