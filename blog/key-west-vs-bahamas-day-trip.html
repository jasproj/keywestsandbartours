Key West to the Bahamas: Can You Do a Day Trip?

The short answer, how people actually get there, and the closest thing to Bahamas water you can reach from Key West in a day

Short answer: no

No ferry, cruise ship or direct flight connects Key West to the Bahamas. To get there you first travel up to Fort Lauderdale or Miami, and that uses most of a day before you board anything bound for the islands. Plan the Bahamas as its own overnight trip. If what you want is clear water and a remote island in one day, book the Dry Tortugas instead.

How people actually get from Key West to the Bahamas

  • Fly. Key West to Fort Lauderdale or Miami is about an hour in the air. Flights from there to Nassau or Bimini take roughly another 40 to 90 minutes, plus the connection.
  • Ferry. Passenger ferries to Bimini and Grand Bahama leave from Fort Lauderdale, not Key West. Each way takes two to three hours.
  • Drive, then ferry. The Overseas Highway to Fort Lauderdale is a long drive on its own. Add a ferry and there is no time left for the islands the same day.
  • Documents. The Bahamas is an international trip, so bring the travel documents your airline or ferry asks for. Nothing in the Florida Keys, including the Dry Tortugas, needs a passport.

The Dry Tortugas: the day trip you're probably looking for

Dry Tortugas National Park sits about 70 miles west of Key West. It's the closest thing to Bahamas-style water you can reach in one day: shallow reef, sand, and Fort Jefferson on Garden Key. It's still the United States, so there's no customs line and no passport.

If you're choosing where to spend the trip

Stay in the Keys if you want to drive in, skip the passport, and fill your days with sandbars, reef snorkeling, sunset sails and Duval Street at night.

Plan a separate Bahamas trip if an international island is the point. Fly or take the ferry from South Florida and give it a few nights. Trying to squeeze it into a Key West vacation mostly buys you time in airports.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you do a Bahamas day trip from Key West?

No. No ferry, cruise ship or direct flight connects Key West to the Bahamas. You would have to travel to Fort Lauderdale or Miami first, then fly or take a ferry, which leaves no time on the islands in the same day.

Is there a ferry from Key West to the Bahamas?

No. Ferries to Bimini and Grand Bahama leave from Fort Lauderdale and take two to three hours each way. The ferry that runs from Key West goes to Fort Myers Beach, not the Bahamas.

What's the closest thing to Bahamas water from Key West?

The Dry Tortugas. The national park is about 70 miles west of Key West, reachable in one day by boat or seaplane, with shallow reef snorkeling around Fort Jefferson. No passport is needed.

Do I need a passport?

For the Bahamas, bring the international travel documents your airline or ferry requires. For anything in the Florida Keys, including the Dry Tortugas, you don't need a passport.
