Key West Bike Tours: Guided Rides & GPS E-Bike Tours

Updated August 2026 • 6 min read

Key West is a bike town. The whole island is about four miles end to end, flat as a dock, and the streets that matter — Old Town's lanes of gingerbread houses, the harbor walk, the beaches on the Atlantic side — are exactly the streets a car can't do anything with. Two wheels at ten miles an hour is the correct speed for this place. There are two ways to do it: join a guided group ride with a local leading and talking, or take a GPS-guided e-bike and follow the tour at your own pace with the bike yours until 5 PM. This guide covers both, with real prices and start times.

Guided or GPS: Which Way to Go?

Want stories told by a person, questions answered, and zero navigation? That's the guided rides — $55 with Key Lime daily-style at 10 AM, or Lloyd's $69/$59 on its select dates. Want the tour plus a free afternoon? The GPS e-bike at $49–$65 is tour and transportation in one booking. Families split the difference by age: the Key Lime ride takes kids 8 and up on their own bikes, while pedal-assist e-bikes suit mixed groups where legs come in different strengths.

Pro Tips for Biking Key West

  1. Book morning starts and mean it. Every ride here starts 9:00–10:00 AM because that's the cool half of the day. Afternoon pedaling in summer is a different sport.
  2. Take the all-day e-bike tier if you're even 60% sure. The gap between the 4-hour and all-day price is small, and having wheels beats hunting parking for the rest of the day.
  3. Bring water and sunscreen anyway. Flat and breezy fools people; four hours in the sun is four hours in the sun.
  4. Check Lloyd's calendar first. It's the one ride that doesn't run daily — lock it in, then plan the rest around it.
  5. Ride like a local. Old Town lanes are shared and slow. The speed limit of the whole experience is "look around."

FAQ

How much do Key West bike tours cost?

Guided rides run $55 per person with Key Lime Bike Tours and $69 adult / $59 child with Lloyd's Tropical Bike Tour. GPS-guided e-bike tours are $49 for four hours or $64.99–$65 to keep the bike all day.

What time do the tours start?

GPS e-bike tours start at 9:00 AM, Lloyd's at 9:30 AM, and the Key Lime guided ride at 10:00 AM.

Can kids ride?

The Key Lime guided tour takes ages 8 and up. Lloyd's prices riders 12 and under at the child rate. The e-bikes are pedal-assist, which flattens the effort for any-age riders.

Do I need to be in shape for these?

No. The island is flat, the pace is easy, and the e-bikes are pedal-assist — the motor does the arguing with the wind.
