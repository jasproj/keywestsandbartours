Fantasy Fest 2026: Boat Parties, Sunset Sails and How to Escape Duval

Fantasy Fest 2026 runs October 16–25. The theme is "Musical Icons and Iconic Musicals," and the parade rolls down Duval Street on Saturday, October 24 at 7 p.m. For ten days the island doubles in population, Duval closes to traffic most nights, and hotels that were booked in March charge what they like.

Here is the part first-timers miss: the festival happens on land after dark. The water is wide open during the day, and a boat is the one place you can escape the crowds, cool off, and still be back on Duval for the night's party. This guide covers what's on, and which boats run during Fantasy Fest with current prices.

Short answer: Book a boat for a daytime slot on the quiet days (Monday–Thursday of festival week) and keep the two big nights, the Masquerade March on Friday the 23rd and the parade on Saturday the 24th, for Duval. Sunset sails on parade night sell out first; private charters on weekday mornings are the last to go.

The festival

Fantasy Fest 2026 at a glance

Dates

Friday, October 16 to Sunday, October 25, 2026.

Theme

Musical Icons and Iconic Musicals.

Parade

Saturday, October 24, 7 p.m., on Duval Street.

Goombay Festival

Opening weekend in Bahama Village: Bahamian food, junkanoo and music. Free and family-friendly.

Masquerade March

Friday, October 23. The Captain Morgan Masquerade March walks from the Key West Cemetery to Duval.

Balls and parties

Pet Masquerade, Headdress Ball and Royal Coronation run across the week. Most signature parties are ticketed, typically $40–$150.

After dark

The last four nights are not for kids. Body paint counts as a costume on Duval after dark.

Dates and the parade time are published by the festival and the Key West Chamber of Commerce; individual party times change year to year, so check fantasyfest.com the week of.

Before you go

Practical notes for festival week

Book boats now

Operators run the same fleet as any other week while the island holds three times the people. Weekend and parade-night slots go first.

Where you board

Most departures are from the Historic Seaport (north end of Duval) or Garrison Bight. Duval closures make driving pointless; walk or use the Duval Loop bus.

Costumes on boats

Operators are relaxed about body paint and costumes on the private charters. Shared sunset sails expect swimwear or clothes.

Weather

Late October is the tail of hurricane season. Operators cancel for weather and refund; your risk is a lost afternoon, not lost money.

Family visit?

Goombay on the opening weekend and any daytime boat are fine for kids. The last four nights on Duval are not.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Fantasy Fest 2026?

Fantasy Fest 2026 runs Friday, October 16 through Sunday, October 25, 2026. The parade is Saturday, October 24 at 7 p.m. on Duval Street. The theme is Musical Icons and Iconic Musicals.

Can you do a boat tour during Fantasy Fest?

Yes. Boats run their normal schedules during Fantasy Fest and the water is far less crowded than Duval Street. Daytime sandbar charters and sunset sails both operate all week; one operator runs a dedicated private, clothing-optional Fantasy Fest charter for up to six guests.

How far ahead should I book a Fantasy Fest boat?

As early as you can. The fleet is the same size as any other week while the island holds several times its usual visitors. Sunset sails on parade night (October 24) and the Friday Masquerade March night sell out first; weekday mornings last longest.

Is Fantasy Fest family-friendly?

The opening weekend's Goombay Festival in Bahama Village is family-friendly, and daytime boat tours are fine for kids all week. The final four nights on Duval Street are adults-only in practice.

What is the Fantasy Fest boat party?

It is a private charter run by Bone Island Charters only during Fantasy Fest: your own boat for up to six people, clothing-optional, cruising the harbor and backcountry. It is priced per boat, so a full group splits the cost.

Get on the water before Duval fills up

Parade-night sunset sails go first. Weekday mornings are the easiest to book.
