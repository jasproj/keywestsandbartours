Best Time to Visit Key West: Complete Seasonal Guide

Check Availability: Sandbar & Dolphin Adventure →
Updated January 2025 • 8 min read

Key West enjoys warm, tropical weather year-round, but timing your visit right can mean the difference between perfect conditions and packed beaches—or hurricane season surprises. Here's exactly when to visit based on weather, crowds, prices, and what you want to do.

Quick Answer: When to Go

🏆 Best Overall: March - May

Perfect weather (75-85°F), calm seas, moderate crowds after spring break. Ideal for water activities. Book 2-3 weeks ahead for popular tours.

👍 Best for Diving/Snorkeling: April - June

Clearest water visibility (60-100ft). Water temp 78-84°F. Calm conditions before summer storms.

💰 Best for Budget: September - November

Lowest prices, fewest crowds. Hurricane risk (manageable with flexibility). Hotel deals up to 40% off.

⚠️ Busiest/Most Expensive: December - February

Peak season, highest prices, biggest crowds. Book months ahead. Great weather but expect lines everywhere.

Seasonal Details

Winter (December - February)

Weather: Warm and dry, 70-80°F. Occasional cold fronts bring temps into the 60s. Water 72-74°F (wetsuits optional).

Crowds: Peak season. Snowbirds escape northern winters. Book accommodations and tours months ahead.

Highlights: Perfect escape from cold weather. Fantasy Fest hangover, holiday events, New Year's celebrations.

Spring (March - May)

Weather: Ideal. 75-85°F, low humidity, minimal rain. Water warms to 82°F by May. Best conditions for water activities.

Crowds: March is spring break madness. April-May perfect—crowds thin but weather stays ideal.

Highlights: Tarpon season begins (April). Best diving visibility. Comfortable for all-day outdoor activities.

Summer (June - August)

Weather: Hot and humid, 88-90°F. Afternoon thunderstorms common (usually brief). Water 85-87°F—like a warm bath.

Crowds: Families arrive for summer vacation. Moderate crowds, good deals.

Highlights: Best water temperatures. Morning activities before afternoon heat. Lobster mini-season (late July).

Fall (September - November)

Weather: Hurricane season peak (Sept-Oct). When storms aren't threatening, weather is beautiful. 79-87°F.

Crowds: Lowest of the year. Locals reclaim the island. Some businesses reduce hours.

Highlights: Fantasy Fest (late October)—Key West's biggest party. Best hotel deals. Fishing excellent.

Best Time for Specific Activities

  • Snorkeling/Diving: April-June (clearest water)
  • Fishing: Year-round, but April-July for tarpon, Nov-March for sailfish
  • Sunset Cruises: Year-round (sunset times vary 6pm-8:30pm)
  • Sandbar Trips: April-October (warmest, calmest)
  • Dolphin Watching: Year-round (morning trips best)
  • Dry Tortugas: March-May (calmest seas)

Timing your time on the water

Key West's water is calmest in the settled months, and that's when sandbar trips are at their best — the flats stay flat and clear. Winter cold fronts can push through and chop the water up for a day or two at a time, while summer runs hot but the water is usually glassy. Whenever you come, both sandbar trips and tiki boats run year-round.

Hurricane Season: Real Talk

Official hurricane season runs June 1 - November 30, with peak activity August-October. Should you avoid Key West during this time?

Not necessarily. Most days are beautiful. Hurricanes are well-forecasted days in advance. The tradeoff: significantly lower prices, virtually no crowds, and flexible booking policies.

Tips for hurricane season travel:

  • Book refundable accommodations
  • Get travel insurance
  • Monitor weather before departure
  • Have a flexible itinerary

FAQ

Key West Timing Questions

What is the best time for a Key West sandbar trip?

Sandbar trips are best in the calmer, settled months when the flats stay flat and clear. Winter cold fronts can chop the water up for a day or two, and summer is hot but usually flat. Trips run year-round.

Do Key West sandbar and tiki boat tours run year-round?

Yes, both run year-round. Sandbar water is calmest in settled weather, and hurricane season from June through November brings the lowest prices and fewest crowds.

When is the best time to visit Key West?

March through May offers the best overall conditions, with warm weather, calm seas, and thinner crowds after spring break. December through February is peak season and the most expensive.

How much is a Key West sandbar trip?

A sandbar and dolphin adventure runs about $129.95 per adult and $89.95 for youth, charged per person.
