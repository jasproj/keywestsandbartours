Tiki Boat vs Private Charter in Key West: Which One Should Your Group Book?

A tiki boat is a private charter — a slow, shaded, floating bar with a captain. The real choice is between a tiki boat and a regular private boat (center console, catamaran, sailboat) for the same money. Tiki wins for a party at the sandbar. A regular boat wins for distance, snorkeling, fishing, or anyone who gets seasick on a slow flat-bottomed barge.

Short answer: Sandbar, drinks, music, photos → tiki boat. Snorkel a reef, see dolphins, go fast, go far, or fish → a real boat. Same price band either way: $525–$799 for six, $1,049+ for twelve.

What a tiki boat actually is

A pontoon-style hull with a thatched roof, a bar top down the middle, seating around the edge, and a small outboard. Top speed is walking pace. It goes to the nearest backcountry sandbar, anchors, and becomes a bar in two feet of water. That is the whole product, and for a bachelor or bachelorette group it is exactly the product. Pick the tiki boat if

  • The plan is the sandbar and nothing else. Floats, music, drinks, four hours.
  • You want shade the whole time. Tiki roofs are the only full shade on the water.
  • You want the photo. The thatched roof at the sandbar is the picture people book Key West for.
  • The group is 7–12 and the budget is $1,049. Nothing else in that size is cheaper.

Pick a regular private charter if

  • Anyone wants to snorkel a real reef. The tiki boat cannot get there.
  • You want dolphins, the Marquesas, or a sunset run — anything with distance.
  • Someone in the group needs a head (bathroom). Book a catamaran.
  • It is windy. Tiki boats stay tied up on days the consoles still run.
  • Half the group fishes. A center console does both; a tiki boat does neither.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a tiki boat a private charter?

Yes. Key West tiki boats are captained private charters — you book the whole boat for your group. Only the tiki sunset cruise on the big catamaran is sold by the seat.

How fast does a tiki boat go?

Walking pace, roughly 5 mph. They go to the nearest backcountry sandbar and anchor. For reefs, dolphins or the Marquesas you need a center console or catamaran.

Do tiki boats have bathrooms?

No. Catamaran charters do. Plan accordingly for a four-hour trip.

Which is cheaper, a tiki boat or a private charter?

For six people the tiki boat ($525 for four hours) is the cheapest private boat in Key West; comparable consoles and catamarans run $650–$799. For twelve, the Salty Bottom tiki boat at $1,049 undercuts the twelve-person catamarans by about $600.
