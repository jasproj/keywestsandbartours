Key West vs Islamorada for Snorkeling: Which to Pick, and What to Book

Same barrier reef, two very different towns. Islamorada is closer to Miami, quieter, and built around the water. Key West has more boats leaving more often, plus everything to do once you're dry. Here's how to choose, with verified trips for both.

Short answer: Pick Islamorada if snorkeling is the point of the trip: it's two hours from Miami instead of four, the reef is a short run out, and the boats are smaller. Pick Key West if you want the reef plus a real town: more departures, easier same-day booking, and sunset sails, Duval Street and Fort Zach when you're done. Both sit on the same Florida Keys reef, and on any given day the wind matters more than the zip code.

The honest version

What actually differs underwater

The reef is the same reef

Both towns sit inside the Florida Keys National Marine Sanctuary on the same barrier reef. Coral heads, parrotfish, angelfish, sergeant majors, rays and the occasional turtle show up at both. Visibility depends on wind and recent weather far more than on which town you booked from.

The difference is the day

Key West boats often run to Sand Key and the Sambos; Islamorada boats to Alligator Reef and Cheeca Rocks. All are shallow, 5 to 25 feet, and beginner-friendly. On a calm summer morning either can be gin-clear. On a windy winter afternoon either can be milky. Book the morning trip wherever you are.

Deciding

Which one is you?

Choose Key West

if this is your first Keys trip, if some of your group won't snorkel, if you want to book the day before, or if the evening matters as much as the morning.

Choose Islamorada

if you're coming from Miami for a day or a weekend, if snorkeling is the reason you're here, if you want small boats and quiet docks, or if you're traveling with kids who are done by 8 PM.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Islamorada or Key West better for snorkeling?

They share the same reef, so the water is comparable. Islamorada is closer to Miami and quieter, with a shorter boat ride to the reef. Key West has more boats, more departures and more to do afterward. Pick by the kind of trip you want, then book a morning departure.

Can you snorkel both on one trip?

Yes. Islamorada is on the way to Key West, about 80 miles up the Overseas Highway. Stop for a morning reef trip on the drive down, or base in Key West and make Islamorada a day trip.

How much does a snorkel trip cost?

Half-day trips start around $99 an adult in both towns. Combo trips that add a sandbar, kayaks or a sunset run $105 to $130.

Do I need experience?

No. Reefs at both are shallow and the boats carry gear, vests and a crew briefing. Kids from about age 4 ride at reduced rates on most trips.

What's the best time of day?

Morning. Wind builds through the afternoon in the Keys and stirs up the water. The morning trips on this page are the safer bet for clear conditions.
