Best Time of Year for a Bachelor or Bachelorette Party in Key West

The best months for a bachelor or bachelorette trip to Key West are late April through June and the first half of November: warm water, calm sandbar days, boats available, and hotel rates a third below March. Fantasy Fest (October 16–25, 2026) is the loudest week of the year and the hardest to book. Here is the year on the water, month by month, and what it does to boat availability.

Short answer: May or early June for the best sandbar weather at the best price. Early November for the same weather with fewer crowds. March–April and Fantasy Fest if the party matters more than the price — book the boat four weeks out.

May and early June: the pick

Water in the low 80s, the flats glass-calm most mornings, the spring-break crowd gone and the summer storms not yet started. The tiki boats and private charters have open weekend slots two weeks out instead of four, and hotels drop 25–35% from March. If you can choose the date, choose this.

Fantasy Fest: what it does to a boat trip

Fantasy Fest is ten days in late October when Key West doubles in population and Duval closes to traffic. The boats are the escape hatch: quiet during the day, back on Duval by dark. Weekday daytime slots are the last to sell; the Saturday parade-night sunset sails are the first. Our Fantasy Fest boat guide has the schedule and which boats run.

Hurricane season: worth the gamble?

August through mid-October is the cheapest Key West of the year for a reason. Most weeks are fine — hot, a 3 p.m. storm, clear by five. But a named storm cancels everything, and captains cancel for wind well before that. If you book in this window, book boats that refund on weather (all the ones on our pages do), morning slots, and travel insurance for the house.

Any month: the two rules

  • Book the boat before the house. Houses in Key West are plentiful; twelve-person tiki boats on a Saturday are not.
  • Morning for the sandbar, evening for the sunset. Wind builds after noon most of the year. Sandbar trips at 10 a.m. are flat; the same trip at 3 p.m. is choppy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best month for a bachelorette party in Key West?

May or early June: warm calm water, boats available one to two weeks out, and hotel rates 25–35% below March. Early November is the second choice — same weather, fewer people.

Is Fantasy Fest a good time for a bachelor party?

For the party, yes; for the boat, book a month ahead and take a weekday daytime slot. Fantasy Fest runs October 16–25, 2026 and the island sells out. Saturday parade-night sunset sails go first.

What happens to a boat booking if a hurricane comes?

The captain cancels and the operator refunds or rebooks — every boat listed on this site follows that policy. Captains also cancel for high wind days before any storm. Book morning slots in August–October and carry travel insurance for the house.

What months are the sandbars best?

Late April through June and early November: water 78–84°F and the lightest winds. Mornings are calmer than afternoons in every month.
